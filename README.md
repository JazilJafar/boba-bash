# Welcome To BOBA Bash In Our CLUB

**About Boba Bash**
 
*Boba Drops is a Hack Club program where your members build personal websites and get rewarded with a grant to buy boba.*

# Features

- **Beginners Friendly**: No prior coding experience is required. We provide step-by-step guidance to help you to create  personal website.

- **Design Your Website**: Customize your website with your own colors, fonts, and images to make it truly yours.

- **Rewards**: Complete the program and receive your boba and winner will get a domain for free.

# **How to Get Started**

 1. **Join the Program**: Sign up for Our club through the Hack Club website and we approve your membership if you meet the requirements.

 2. **Come to the Boba Bash**: Attend our Boba Bash event where we will teach you how to make your own personal website in 14/11/2026.

 3. **Deploy Your Website**: Once you've built your website, we will help you deploy it online.

 4. **Get Your Boba** : After successfully deploying your website, you will receive your boba and winner will get a domain for free.
